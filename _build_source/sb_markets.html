How It Works

From Enquiry to Bill of Lading

01

Enquiry & quotation

Send your product codes and quantities. You get a costed quotation with the applicable certificates attached, usually within one business day.

02

Sampling & approval

Pre-production samples for new specifications or private-label artwork, couriered for approval before the line is committed.

03

Manufacture & testing

Production runs in-house across cutting, stitching and assembly, with batch testing and in-line inspection at every stage.

04

Documentation & dispatch

Export packing, invoice, packing list, certificate of origin and test reports issued together, then FOB or CIF as agreed.

Trade Terms

The Commercial Detail

Incoterms
FOB, CIF and CFR — Nhava Sheva, Mundra or Kolkata
Payment
Letter of credit at sight, or advance TT for first orders
Lead time
3–5 weeks for catalogue lines, 6–8 for private label
MOQ
Container or mixed-pallet quantities; ask about trial orders
Labelling
Your brand, artwork and languages, applied in-house
Packing
Master cartons to your carton specification and marking

What ships with the goods

  • Commercial invoice and packing list
  • Certificate of origin (Council for Leather Exports)
  • EN / ANSI third-party type-test reports
  • ISO 9001:2015 certificate of conformity
  • Batch-level declaration of conformity
  • SEDEX audit reference on request

Product-level test documentation is issued with the quotation, not after the order — so your compliance team can sign off before you commit.
