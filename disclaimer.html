3. Pre-Existing Structural Defects & Tank Integrity

Septic tanks, cesspools, soak pits, and underground inspection chambers are subject to progressive structural wear, corrosive sewer gases (hydrogen sulfide), root intrusion from nearby trees, and masonry decay.

Green Septic Tank Cleaning Services operates high-power vacuum suction equipment designed for liquid and sludge extraction. We disclaim any liability for:

  • The collapse, cracking, or settlement of pre-existing fragile, unreinforced, corroded, or sub-standard masonry walls, baffle walls, or chamber slabs.
  • Damage to rusted or degraded manhole frames and thin unreinforced concrete lids during necessary opening and closing operations.
  • Pre-existing underground pipeline breaks, root-damaged sewer connections, or blockages originating within non-accessible private wall piping.
