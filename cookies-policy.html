Cookies Policy

How Green Septic Tank Cleaning Services uses cookies and lightweight browser technologies to enhance your experience.

Last Updated: November 22, 2026

This Cookies Policy explains how Green Septic Tank Cleaning Services uses cookies, web storage, and related browser technologies when you visit our website. We believe in complete transparency regarding how and why website data is collected.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your computer, smartphone, or tablet by your web browser when you visit a website. They allow websites to recognize your device, maintain essential interactive features, remember your display preferences, and understand which sections of the site provide the greatest utility to users.

2. Categories of Cookies We Use

Our website utilizes a minimal, performance-focused set of cookies categorized as follows:

  • Strictly Necessary Cookies: These technical cookies are fundamental for navigating our website and utilizing basic features, such as our mobile navigation menu, instant-call buttons, and secure contact forms. Without these, core website functionality cannot be provided.
  • Performance & Analytics Cookies: These anonymous metrics help us evaluate page load speeds, verify server response times, monitor broken link occurrences, and assess user interaction with service sections. All data is aggregated and does not personally identify you.
  • Functionality & Preference Cookies: These cookies store lightweight preferences, such as keeping mobile drawer status consistent or remembering accessibility adjustments (e.g. reduced-motion preferences).

3. Third-Party Integrations

To provide responsive emergency dispatch and accurate mapping, certain third-party services on our website may set cookies on your device:

  • Google Maps & Fonts: We use Google Fonts for responsive typography and embedded Google Maps for geographic locality reference across Hyderabad. Google may set cookies in accordance with its privacy policies.
  • WhatsApp Direct Connect: When clicking our floating WhatsApp button to chat with our dispatch desk, you are redirected to WhatsApp's secure portal, which operates under Meta's privacy and cookie protocols.

4. How to Manage or Disable Cookies

You have full control over cookie permissions through your web browser settings. Most modern browsers allow you to view, manage, delete, or completely block cookies:

  • Google Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Third-party cookies.
  • Mozilla Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data.
  • Apple Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data.
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions.

Please note: Disabling technical cookies will never prevent you from calling our direct 24/7 emergency dispatch (Call Now).

5. Updates to This Cookies Policy

We may update this policy periodically to reflect operational, legal, or regulatory modifications. Any updates will be posted directly to this page with a revised "Last Updated" timestamp.
