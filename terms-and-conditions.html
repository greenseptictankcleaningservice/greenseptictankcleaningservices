Terms & Conditions

Operational guidelines, customer site responsibilities, transparent pricing, and sanitation safety agreements across Hyderabad.

Last Updated: November 22, 2026

Welcome to Green Septic Tank Cleaning Services. These Terms and Conditions govern all sanitation bookings, septic tank pumping, vacuum sludge suction, STP maintenance, and drainage blockage clearance services provided across Greater Hyderabad, Secunderabad, and Ranga Reddy district.

1. Acceptance of Service Terms

By booking our services via telephone (call now), WhatsApp messaging, or our online quote form, you accept and agree to be legally bound by these Terms and Conditions.

2. Scope of Services

Green Septic Tank Cleaning Services provides mechanized liquid waste management solutions, including:

  • Heavy-duty vacuum tanker suction pumping for residential septic tanks and commercial holding tanks.
  • Liquid sewage sludge and sediment evacuation for gated communities, apartments, and villa projects.
  • Sewage Treatment Plant (STP) chamber cleaning and sludge cake extraction.
  • High-pressure hydro-jetting for internal drainage pipe and manhole blockage clearance.
  • Authorized transit and offloading of collected septage strictly at designated municipal sewage treatment facilities.

3. Prohibited Waste & Hazardous Materials

Our fleet, vacuum equipment, and disposal licenses are certified strictly for domestic, commercial, and sanitary septage. We do not accept and will strictly refuse hazardous chemical effluents, toxic industrial acids, radioactive substances, medical bio-waste, petroleum fuels, or volatile solvents. The customer agrees to hold us harmless and assumes full civil and criminal liability for any undeclared hazardous materials introduced into the septic system.

4. Site Access & Customer Obligations

To facilitate efficient and safe vacuum suction operations, the customer agrees to:

  • Clear Tanker Access: Provide unobstructed access for our commercial vacuum suction trucks to park within operational distance of the septic tank or manhole chamber.
  • Hose Routing: Ensure a clear, safe pathway for vacuum suction hoses through gates, driveways, or gardens. Standard hose length covers up to 50–100 feet; hose runs exceeding 100 feet must be notified in advance and may incur supplementary hose handling fees.
  • Tank Identification & Manhole Opening: Point out the exact location of the septic tank and assist in providing access to the manhole cover. Pre-existing brittle covers, rusted metal frames, or cracked cement slabs should be handled with mutual care.

5. Upfront Pricing, Estimates & Payment

All phone, message, or online quotes are provisional estimates based on customer-reported tank capacities. Final charges are determined on-site according to:

  • Verified tank capacity in liters or number of tanker loads extracted.
  • Depth and density of solidified bottom sludge requiring manual dilution, agitation, or heavy suction.
  • Excessive hose extension distance or difficult multi-level pipe ascents.

Payment Terms: Full payment is due immediately upon completion of the suction job. We accept Cash, UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM), and direct IMPS/NEFT bank transfers.

6. 100% Mechanized Safety (Zero Manual Scavenging)

In strict compliance with the Prohibition of Employment as Manual Scavengers and their Rehabilitation Act, 2013 and Supreme Court of India directives, under no circumstances will our technicians enter an unventilated septic tank, cesspool, or manhole chamber. All sludge removal is performed exclusively using mechanical high-vacuum suction wands and pressurized water jets from outside the tank.

7. Cancellations & Rescheduling

We understand emergencies and scheduling changes happen. You may cancel or reschedule your service without penalty before our suction tanker departs our depot. In instances where a tanker has already arrived at the customer's location and service is canceled due to customer-side factors (e.g. lack of tank access, property dispute), a nominal trip dispatch fee may apply.

8. Limitation of Liability

Green Septic Tank Cleaning Services uses well-maintained machinery and experienced personnel. However, we cannot be held liable for:

  • Pre-existing structural fractures, masonry collapse, or crumbling walls of aged, unreinforced, or poorly constructed septic tanks.
  • Plumbing line blockages caused by non-flushable debris (such as sanitary towels, plastic wrappers, construction cement, or invasive tree root penetrations) inside building pipes.
  • Delays caused by severe traffic congestion, road closures, or adverse weather conditions across Greater Hyderabad.

9. Governing Law & Jurisdiction

These Terms and Conditions shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Republic of India. Any legal dispute or claim arising under this agreement shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the competent courts in Hyderabad or Ranga Reddy District, Telangana.
